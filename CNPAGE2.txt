Types of Data flow:  
1.Simplex- One way communication(Eg- Keyboard to CPU).
2.Half-Duplex- Two way communication, but only one direction at a time.(Eg- Walkie-Talkie).
3.Full-Duplex- Two way communication simultaneously.(Eg- Telephone).

Personal area network(PAN)
It connects personal devices within a short range.
Ex-Bluetooth
Technology- Bluetooth,Irda, Zigbee
Range- 1-100m
Transmission Speed- Very High
Ownership- Private
Area- Within a room
Maintenance- Very easy
Error rate and cost- Very low

Local Area Network(LAN)
It connects within a small area, like an office or building.
Ex-School
Technology-Ethernet, Wi-fi
Range- upto 2km
Transmission Speed- Very High
Ownership- Private
Area- Within office building
Maintenance- Easy
Error rate and cost- low

Campus Area Network(CAN)
It connects networks within campus or university.
Ex-University
Technology-Ethernet, Wi-fi, Fiber optics
Range- 1-5km
Transmission Speed- High
Ownership- Private
Area- Within university,office
Maintenance- Moderate
Error rate and cost- Moderate

Metropolitan Area Network(MAN)
It connects networks across a city.
Ex- City
Technology-Metro Ethernet, MPLS
Range- 5-50KM
Transmission Speed- Average
Ownership- Private/Public
Area- Within cities like Mumbai
Maintenance- Difficult
Error rate and cost- High

Wide Area Network(WAN)
It connects networks accross countries or the world.
Ex- Internet
Technology-Leased Line, VPN, MPLS, Satellite
Range- Avove 50km to global range
Transmission Speed- Low
Ownership- Private/Public
Area- Within countries
Maintenance- Very Difficult
Error rate and cost- Very High
