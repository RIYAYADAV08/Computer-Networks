Types of Data flow:
Simplex- One way communication
Eg-Keyboard to CPU   

Half-Duplex- Two way communication but only one direction at a time.
Eg- Walkie-Talkie

Full-Duplex- Two way communication simultaneously.
Eg- Telephone

PAN(Personal Area Network)

LAN(Local Area Network)
A network inside one building or office.
Technology- Ethernet, Wi-Fi
Range- Upto 2 km.
Transmission speed- Very high
Area- Within office bhuilding

Maintenance- Easy
